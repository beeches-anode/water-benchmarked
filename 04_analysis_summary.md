# Phase 5: Benchmarking Analysis Summary

**Analysis Date:** January 2026
**Data Currency:** AUD 2024 (Real)
**Version:** 2.0

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines **10 bulk water pipeline projects** from Queensland, Victoria, and the Northern Territory with construction activity between 2019-2025. The core sample of **8 large-diameter (≥300mm) municipal bulk supply pipelines** provides the primary benchmarking basis, with 2 supplementary projects included for context.

**Key Finding:** Large-diameter bulk water pipeline costs in the 2019-2025 period range from **$2.5M to $14.3M per kilometre** (AUD 2024), with significant variation driven by diameter, terrain complexity, scope inclusions, and market conditions.

### Version 2.0 Improvements
- Expanded sample from 6 to 10 projects
- Added Northern Territory coverage (first NT project)
- Increased completed project count from 2 to 5
- Improved medium-diameter (300-600mm) coverage with 4 additional data points

## 8. Limitations & Caveats

### 8.1 Sample Limitations

| Limitation | Impact | Mitigation |
|------------|--------|------------|
| Sample size (n=8-10) | Moderate statistical power | Present ranges, not point estimates |
| Geographic concentration (70% QLD) | May not generalise nationally | Note regional specificity |
| Time period volatility | Costs may not persist | Document market conditions |
| Scope variation | Impairs comparability | Adjust for major inclusions |
| Data gaps (YYB cost, MDP breakdown) | Reduces confidence | Flag estimated values |
