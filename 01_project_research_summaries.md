# Bulk Water Pipeline Benchmarking Study
## Individual Project Research Summaries

**Prepared:** January 2026
**Scope:** Bulk water supply pipelines (≥300mm diameter) - Queensland priority, broader Australia
**Timeframe:** Construction completed or substantially commenced 2019-2025

## Project 1: Fitzroy to Gladstone Pipeline (Queensland)

### Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Fitzroy to Gladstone Pipeline (FGP)
- **Location:** Rockhampton to Yarwun/Gladstone, Central Queensland
- **Delivery Authority:** Gladstone Area Water Board (GAWB)
- **Construction Contractor:** McConnell Dowell BMD Joint Venture (MBJV)

### Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 117 km
- **Diameter:** 1,000 mm (1.0 m)
- **Capacity:** 30,000 ML/annum (30 GL/year)
- **Daily Transfer Capacity:** ~82 ML/day at full capacity

### Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Total Capital Cost** | $983,000,000 | 2023 (nominal) | Queensland Government funded |
| 2024-25 Budget Allocation | $311,000,000 | 2024 | State budget item |
| Local Spend to Date | $71,600,000 | 2024 | Central Queensland businesses |

### Cost Metrics (Nominal)
- **$/km:** $8,402,564/km
- **$/ML annual capacity:** $32,767/ML

### Key Scope Features
- Water treatment plant included in scope
- Two 50ML reservoirs (76m diameter, 12m high each)
- Pumping stations along route
- Connects to existing GAWB network at Yarwun
- Predominantly rural/agricultural terrain
- River crossings required

### Delivery Model
- **Procurement:** Early Contractor Involvement (ECI) competitive process
- **Contract Type:** Design and Construct (D&C) with ECI
- 58 innovations identified during ECI phase to drive value

### Timeline
- **Approval:** 23 February 2023
- **Construction Start:** 2023
- **Halfway Point:** June 2024 (63km laid)
- **Expected Completion:** 2026

### Employment & Economic Impact
- ~400 workers at peak construction
- 25 apprenticeships created
- 111 Central Queensland businesses engaged

### Primary Sources
1. Gladstone Area Water Board - Project Page: https://www.gawb.qld.gov.au/projects/fitzroy-to-gladstone-pipeline/
2. Queensland Government Ministerial Statement (Feb 2023): https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/97228
3. McConnell Dowell Project Profile: https://www.mcconnelldowell.com/projects/fitzroy-to-gladstone-pipeline

## Project 2: Haughton Pipeline Stage 1 (Queensland)

### Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Haughton Pipeline Duplication Project - Stage 1
- **Location:** Haughton River to Ross River Dam, Townsville
- **Delivery Authority:** Townsville City Council
- **Funding Partners:** Queensland Government, Council

### Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 36 km (33km of pipe, plus connections)
- **Diameter:** 1,800 mm (1.8 m)
- **Pipe Material:** Glass Reinforced Polymer (GRP)
- **Pipe Sections:** 2,763 individual lengths

### Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Total Capital Cost** | $215,000,000 | 2017 (nominal) | Queensland Government primary funder |
| Additional Water Smart Package | $10,000,000 | 2017 | Complementary investment |

### Cost Metrics (Nominal)
- **$/km:** $5,972,222/km

### Key Scope Features
- Large diameter GRP pipeline (1.8m)
- Pumping from Haughton Channel to Ross River Dam
- Duplicates existing supply infrastructure
- Relatively flat terrain
- Agricultural/rural corridor

### Delivery Model
- Traditional government-funded council delivery
- Staged approach allowing for demand-driven expansion

### Timeline
- **Funding Secured:** 2017
- **Construction:** 2018-2020

## Project 3: Haughton Pipeline Stage 2 (Queensland)

### Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Haughton Pipeline Duplication Project - Stage 2
- **Location:** Haughton River to Burdekin River (Clare), Townsville region
- **Delivery Authority:** Townsville City Council
- **Funding Partners:** Queensland Government ($195M), Council ($79M+)

### Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 28.2 km (28.5 km in some sources)
- **Diameter:** 1,800 mm (1.8 m)
- **Pipe Material:** Glass Reinforced Polymer (GRP)
- **Pipe Supply Contract:** $51M to Iplex (24km of 1.8m GRP)
- **Capacity:** 356 ML/day when combined with Stage 1

### Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Original Estimate** | $179,600,000 | 2019 | Business case estimate |
| **Revised Budget** | $274,000,000 | 2021 | With contingency |
| Queensland Government | $195,000,000 | 2021 | State contribution |
| Council Contribution | $79,000,000 | 2021 | Local government share |
| **Reported Outturn (2025)** | $420,000,000+ | 2025 | Per media reports |
| Pipe Supply Contract | $51,000,000 | 2023 | Iplex Australia |

### Cost Metrics (Based on $274M approved budget)
- **$/km:** $9,716,312/km

### Key Scope Features
- Extension through greenfield terrain
- 6km of construction access roads required
- Connects to Burdekin River water source
- Large diameter GRP pipeline continuation
- Pump station upgrades

### Delivery Model
- Government-funded council delivery
- Traditional procurement
- Construction contract separate from pipe supply

### Timeline
- **Business Case:** October 2019
- **Funding Confirmed:** 2021
- **Construction Start:** October 2023
- **Progress (Oct 2024):** 27km of 28.2km laid (96%)
- **Expected Completion:** 2025 (delayed from original)

### Cost Escalation Factors
- COVID-19 pandemic impacts on construction costs
- Material price increases
- Labour cost escalation
- Original budget acknowledged as understated

## Project 4: Toowoomba to Warwick Pipeline (Queensland)

### Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Toowoomba to Warwick Pipeline (TWP)
- **Location:** Toowoomba to Warwick via Cambooya, Greenmount, Nobby, Clifton, Southern Downs
- **Delivery Authority:** Seqwater
- **Asset Owner:** Seqwater (post-construction)

### Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 109-111 km (varies by source)
- **Diameter:** 350-600 mm (variable along route)
- **Daily Capacity:** 7.3-10.3 ML/day
- **Connection:** Links to Wivenhoe-Cressbrook Pipeline at Toowoomba end

### Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Initial Announcement** | $300,000,000 | 2022 | Initial budget |
| **Revised Estimate** | $370,000,000+ | 2023 | Updated figures |
| **Current Estimate** | $467,000,000 | 2024 | Cost escalation reported |
| Pre-construction/Feasibility | $19,340,000 | 2021 | Early works package |

### Cost Metrics (Based on $370M mid-range)
- **$/km:** $3,333,333/km (at $370M/111km)

### Key Scope Features
- Drought contingency pipeline (not continuous supply)
- Underground construction through agricultural land
- Water treatment facilities along route
- Network monitoring systems
- Connection to existing Wivenhoe infrastructure
- Serves 24,000+ residents in drought conditions
- Traverses challenging terrain including Cunningham's Gap approach

### Communities Served
- Cambooya, Greenmount, Nobby, Clifton (along route)
- Warwick, Allora, Yangan (drought contingency)
- Potential carting to Stanthorpe, Killarney

### Delivery Model
- **Procurement:** Market Expression of Interest (EOI)
- **Contract:** Seeking qualified head contractor
- **Award Expected:** Late 2025
- **Approach:** Head contractor with local subcontractor requirements

### Timeline
- **Feasibility Funding:** April 2021
- **Direction to Seqwater:** April 2023
- **Pre-construction Start:** 2024
- **Contract Award Target:** Late 2025
- **Expected Completion:** 2027

### Primary Sources

2. Queensland DLGWV: https://www.dlgwv.qld.gov.au/water/consultations-initiatives/toowoomba-warwick-pipeline

## Project 5: Mareeba-Dimbulah Water Supply Scheme Efficiency Improvement (Queensland)

### Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Mareeba-Dimbulah Water Supply Scheme Efficiency Improvement Project
- **Location:** Atherton Tablelands, Far North Queensland
- **Delivery Authority:** Sunwater
- **Funding Partners:** Australian Government ($11.6M), Queensland Government via Sunwater ($20.9M)

### Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Replaced:** 14 km
- **Diameter:** Not specified (irrigation supply pipeline)
- **Additional Scope:** 125 automated channel control gates, 157 water offtake upgrades

### Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Total Project Cost** | $32,540,000 | 2021 | Final cost |
| Australian Government | $11,600,000 | - | National Water Grid Fund |
| Queensland Government/Sunwater | $20,940,000 | - | State/utility contribution |

### Cost Metrics
- **$/km (pipeline only):** $2,324,286/km (notional, includes other works)

### Key Scope Features
- Pipeline replacement within existing scheme
- Automation of irrigation controls
- Water efficiency improvements
- Existing corridor/alignment
- Agricultural/irrigation focus

### Outcomes
- Water savings: 11,500 ML/annum (exceeded 8,306 ML target by 38%)
- Economic benefit: $20M/year additional agricultural production
- First new water for permanent purchase since 1990s

### Delivery Model
- Sunwater direct delivery
- National Water Grid Fund partnership

### Timeline
- **Construction Period:** 2020-2021
- **Completion:** December 2021

## Project 6: East Grampians Rural Pipeline (Victoria)

### Project Identification
- **Project Name:** East Grampians Rural Pipeline Project
- **Location:** Grampians region, Western Victoria
- **Delivery Authority:** GWMWater (Grampians Wimmera Mallee Water)
- **Construction Contractor:** Mitchell Water Australia

### Technical Specifications
- **Total Pipeline Network:** ~600 km (trunk + distribution)
- **Trunk Mains:** ~130-250 km of 100-450 mm diameter
- **Distribution Mains:** ~470-1,100 km of 63-125 mm diameter
- **Coverage Area:** 530,000 hectares
- **Connections:** Up to 1,500 rural properties

### Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Total Confirmed Funding** | $30,600,000 | 2024 | Current confirmed budget |
| Victorian Government | $15,000,000 | 2024 | State contribution |
| Australian Government | $32,000,000 | - | National Water Grid Fund |
| GWMWater Contribution | $15,600,000 | - | Utility investment |
| **Potential Total** | ~$64,000,000 | - | If all funding secured |

### Cost Metrics (Based on $64M total, 130km trunk)
- **$/km (trunk only):** ~$492,308/km

### Key Scope Features
- Rural stock and domestic supply (not bulk municipal)
- Lake Fyans primary water source
- Mt William headworks and Mt Cole Reservoir secondary sources
- Pump station network (~4 stations)
- 1,000 kW solar panel installations
- Lake Fyans pump station upgrade
- Rural/agricultural terrain

### Capacity
- Annual Supply: 1,400 ML to rural properties
- Properties Served: ~600 rural businesses initially

### Delivery Model
- GWMWater project management
- Construction contract to Mitchell Water Australia
- Joint government funding model

### Timeline
- **Planning/Approvals:** 2021
- **Construction Start:** 2023
- **Current Status:** Lake Fyans Pump Station and >100km pipe installed
- **Expected Completion:** Late 2026

### Primary Sources
1. GWMWater Project Page: https://www.gwmwater.org.au/our-water-supply/current-projects/east-grampians-rural-pipeline-project
2. National Water Grid Authority: https://www.nationalwatergrid.gov.au/projects/east-grampians-rural-water-supply-project
