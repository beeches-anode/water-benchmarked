## Projects Identified But Excluded From Primary Analysis

### Burdekin to Moranbah Pipeline (Queensland)
- **Cost:** $270-280 million
- **Length:** 216-218 km
- **Completion:** 2006-2007
- **Reason for Exclusion:** Completed prior to 2019 timeframe

### Adelaide Desalination Transfer Pipeline (South Australia)
- **Cost:** Part of $1.824 billion desalination project
- **Length:** 12 km
- **Diameter:** 1.5 m
- **Completion:** 2011-2013
- **Reason for Exclusion:** Completed prior to 2019 timeframe; desalination plant internal infrastructure excluded per scope

### Victorian Desalination Pipeline
- **Cost:** Part of $3.5 billion project
- **Length:** 84 km
- **Completion:** Pre-2019
- **Reason for Exclusion:** Completed prior to 2019 timeframe

### Dungowan Dam and Pipeline (NSW)
- **Original Budget:** $1.28 billion (dam + pipeline)
- **Pipeline Length:** 55 km
- **Status:** Cancelled following negative business case assessment
- **Reason for Exclusion:** Project cancelled, not constructed

### Goldfields Pipeline Stage 1 (Western Australia)
- **Budget:** $543 million
- **Length:** 44.5 km replacement
- **Status:** Construction to commence mid-2026
- **Reason for Exclusion:** Construction not yet commenced within timeframe
