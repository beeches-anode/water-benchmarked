## Yan Yean WTP Upgrade Context
The pipeline is part of a larger program to upgrade and expand Melbourne's northern water supply system:
- Yan Yean WTP upgraded to modern standards
- New pump station constructed at Yan Yean
- 20 km pipeline to Bald Hill to deliver increased capacity
- Supports population growth in northern Melbourne suburbs
- Improves supply reliability for Whittlesea, Mitchell, Nillumbik areas

## Technical Complexity

### Gradient Challenge
The pipeline traverses challenging terrain including a 29% gradient at the Bald Hill section. This steep section requires:
- High-pressure rating pipe (PN35)
- Specialised installation techniques
- Thrust blocks and anchor points
- Additional engineering for pipe stability

### Tunnelling Works
Micro-tunnel boring was used for:
- **Merri Creek crossing** - Environmentally sensitive waterway
- **Railway crossing** - Avoiding disruption to rail services
- These trenchless methods add significant cost but reduce environmental and community impact
