# Mount Morgan Pipeline (Queensland)

**Project Code:** P7-MMP
**State:** Queensland
**Status:** Completed (Testing/Commissioning 2024)

---

## Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Mount Morgan Water Supply Pipeline
- **Location:** Gracemere to Mount Morgan, Central Queensland
- **Delivery Authority:** Rockhampton Regional Council
- **Funding Partners:** Australian Government, Queensland Government, Rockhampton Regional Council

## Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 28 km
- **Diameter:** Estimated 300-450mm (requires verification)
- **Pipe Material:** Not specified (likely DICL or steel - requires verification)
- **Design Capacity:** High-pressure treated water supply to Mount Morgan township

## Key Scope Features
- 28 km pipeline from Gracemere to Mount Morgan
- 2 new reservoirs at Gracemere (3.65ML capacity each)
- 1 new reservoir at Moonmera (0.5ML capacity)
- Multiple pump stations along route
- High-pressure treated water supply system
- Challenging terrain including Razorback range crossing
- Replaces century-old water supply infrastructure

## Timeline
- **Announcement/Funding Secured:** 2021-2022
- **Construction Start:** 2022

- **Fully Operational:** Late 2024/Early 2025

## Project Objectives
- Provide reliable, safe drinking water to Mount Morgan residents
- Replace aging infrastructure that had reached end of useful life
- Eliminate reliance on local water sources of declining quality
- Support community growth and economic development
- Connect to modern treated water supply network

## Employment & Economic Impact
- Local and regional construction employment
- Support for Mount Morgan township (~2,000 residents)
- Restoration of water security to historical mining community
