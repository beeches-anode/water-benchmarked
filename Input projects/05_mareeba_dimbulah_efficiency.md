# Mareeba-Dimbulah Water Supply Scheme Efficiency Improvement (Queensland)

**Project Code:** P5-MDWSS
**State:** Queensland
**Status:** Completed (December 2021)

---

## Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Mareeba-Dimbulah Water Supply Scheme Efficiency Improvement Project
- **Location:** Atherton Tablelands, Far North Queensland
- **Delivery Authority:** Sunwater
- **Funding Partners:** Australian Government ($11.6M), Queensland Government via Sunwater ($20.9M)

## Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Replaced:** 14 km
- **Diameter:** Not specified (irrigation supply pipeline)
- **Additional Scope:** 125 automated channel control gates, 157 water offtake upgrades

## Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Total Project Cost** | $32,540,000 | 2021 | Final cost |
| Australian Government | $11,600,000 | - | National Water Grid Fund |
| Queensland Government/Sunwater | $20,940,000 | - | State/utility contribution |

## Cost Metrics
- **$/km (pipeline only):** $2,324,286/km (notional, includes other works)

## Key Scope Features
- Pipeline replacement within existing scheme
- Automation of irrigation controls
- Water efficiency improvements
- Existing corridor/alignment
- Agricultural/irrigation focus

## Outcomes
- Water savings: 11,500 ML/annum (exceeded 8,306 ML target by 38%)
- Economic benefit: $20M/year additional agricultural production
- First new water for permanent purchase since 1990s

## Delivery Model
- Sunwater direct delivery
- National Water Grid Fund partnership

## Timeline
- **Construction Period:** 2020-2021
- **Completion:** December 2021
