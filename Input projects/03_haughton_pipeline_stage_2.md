# Haughton Pipeline Stage 2 (Queensland)

**Project Code:** P3-HP2
**State:** Queensland
**Status:** Under construction

---

## Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Haughton Pipeline Duplication Project - Stage 2
- **Location:** Haughton River to Burdekin River (Clare), Townsville region
- **Delivery Authority:** Townsville City Council
- **Funding Partners:** Queensland Government ($195M), Council ($79M+)

## Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 28.2 km (28.5 km in some sources)
- **Diameter:** 1,800 mm (1.8 m)
- **Pipe Material:** Glass Reinforced Polymer (GRP)
- **Pipe Supply Contract:** $51M to Iplex (24km of 1.8m GRP)
- **Capacity:** 356 ML/day when combined with Stage 1

## Cost Metrics (Based on $274M approved budget)
- **$/km:** $9,716,312/km

## Key Scope Features
- Extension through greenfield terrain
- 6km of construction access roads required
- Connects to Burdekin River water source
- Large diameter GRP pipeline continuation
- Pump station upgrades

## Delivery Model
- Government-funded council delivery
- Traditional procurement
- Construction contract separate from pipe supply

## Timeline
- **Business Case:** October 2019
- **Funding Confirmed:** 2021
- **Construction Start:** October 2023
- **Progress (Oct 2024):** 27km of 28.2km laid (96%)
- **Expected Completion:** 2025 (delayed from original)

## Cost Escalation Factors
- COVID-19 pandemic impacts on construction costs
- Material price increases
- Labour cost escalation
- Original budget acknowledged as understated
