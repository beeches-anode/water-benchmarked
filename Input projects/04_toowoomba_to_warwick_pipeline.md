# Toowoomba to Warwick Pipeline (Queensland)

**Project Code:** P4-TWP
**State:** Queensland
**Status:** Pre-construction

---

## Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Toowoomba to Warwick Pipeline (TWP)
- **Location:** Toowoomba to Warwick via Cambooya, Greenmount, Nobby, Clifton, Southern Downs
- **Delivery Authority:** Seqwater
- **Asset Owner:** Seqwater (post-construction)

## Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 109-111 km (varies by source)
- **Diameter:** 350-600 mm (variable along route)
- **Daily Capacity:** 7.3-10.3 ML/day
- **Connection:** Links to Wivenhoe-Cressbrook Pipeline at Toowoomba end

## Cost Metrics (Based on $370M mid-range)
- **$/km:** $3,333,333/km (at $370M/111km)

## Key Scope Features
- Drought contingency pipeline (not continuous supply)
- Underground construction through agricultural land
- Water treatment facilities along route
- Network monitoring systems
- Connection to existing Wivenhoe infrastructure
- Serves 24,000+ residents in drought conditions
- Traverses challenging terrain including Cunningham's Gap approach

## Communities Served
- Cambooya, Greenmount, Nobby, Clifton (along route)
- Warwick, Allora, Yangan (drought contingency)
- Potential carting to Stanthorpe, Killarney

## Delivery Model
- **Procurement:** Market Expression of Interest (EOI)
- **Contract:** Seeking qualified head contractor
- **Award Expected:** Late 2025
- **Approach:** Head contractor with local subcontractor requirements

## Timeline
- **Feasibility Funding:** April 2021
- **Direction to Seqwater:** April 2023
- **Pre-construction Start:** 2024
- **Contract Award Target:** Late 2025
- **Expected Completion:** 2027
