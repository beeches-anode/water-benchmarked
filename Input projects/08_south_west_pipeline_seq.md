## SEQ Water Grid Context
The South West Pipeline is a significant expansion of the SEQ Water Grid, which is one of Australia's most interconnected urban water supply systems. The pipeline:
- Connects the Beaudesert region to the broader grid for the first time
- Enables water to flow from multiple sources (Hinze Dam, Gold Coast sources) to Beaudesert
- Allows surplus water to flow from Beaudesert to other parts of the grid
- Represents the biggest grid addition since the Hinze Dam to Molendinar connection in 2012
