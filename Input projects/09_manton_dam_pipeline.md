## Darwin Region Water Supply Program Context
The Manton Dam Return to Service Pipeline is the first stage of a $327.6M Darwin Region Water Supply Infrastructure Program. The program:
- Responds to growing Darwin population and climate change
- Restores Manton Dam as a supply source (dam was taken offline)
- Builds new treatment capacity at Strauss
- Complements existing Darwin River Dam and McMinns WTP sources
- Provides supply diversification and drought resilience
