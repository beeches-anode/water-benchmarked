# Haughton Pipeline Stage 1 (Queensland)

**Project Code:** P2-HP1
**State:** Queensland
**Status:** Completed (June 2020)

---

## Project Identification
- **Project Name:** Haughton Pipeline Duplication Project - Stage 1
- **Location:** Haughton River to Ross River Dam, Townsville
- **Delivery Authority:** Townsville City Council
- **Funding Partners:** Queensland Government, Council

## Technical Specifications
- **Pipeline Length:** 36 km (33km of pipe, plus connections)
- **Diameter:** 1,800 mm (1.8 m)
- **Pipe Material:** Glass Reinforced Polymer (GRP)
- **Pipe Sections:** 2,763 individual lengths

## Project Costs
| Cost Component | Amount (AUD) | Year | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|------|-------|
| **Total Capital Cost** | $215,000,000 | 2017 (nominal) | Queensland Government primary funder |
| Additional Water Smart Package | $10,000,000 | 2017 | Complementary investment |

## Cost Metrics (Nominal)
- **$/km:** $5,972,222/km

## Key Scope Features
- Large diameter GRP pipeline (1.8m)
- Pumping from Haughton Channel to Ross River Dam
- Duplicates existing supply infrastructure
- Relatively flat terrain
- Agricultural/rural corridor

## Delivery Model
- Traditional government-funded council delivery
- Staged approach allowing for demand-driven expansion

## Timeline
- **Funding Secured:** 2017
- **Construction:** 2018-2020
- **Completion:** June 2020
