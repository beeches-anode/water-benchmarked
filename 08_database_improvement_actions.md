### E2. Template Information Request Letter

```
Subject: Water Pipeline Benchmarking Study - Data Request

Dear [Name],

I am conducting a benchmarking study of bulk water pipeline infrastructure
costs across Australia to support improved cost estimation for future projects.

The study has identified [Project Name] as a valuable reference project.
I would be grateful if you could provide the following information:

1. Total final/current project cost (and cost base year)
2. Pipeline specifications:
   - Total length (km)
   - Diameter(s) (mm)
   - Pipe material
3. Key scope inclusions (pump stations, reservoirs, treatment, etc.)
4. Any cost breakdown by major component

This information will be used to develop industry benchmarks and will be
appropriately attributed to [Organisation] as the source.

Thank you for your assistance.

[Signature]
```
